## Overview

**Creative North Star: "FoodDelivery — clear shopping"**

A concise shopping interface: real food photography, clear prices and available stock. Emerald carries navigation and shopping feedback; amber marks the main shopping action. Short Vietnamese labels replace promotional paragraphs.

This records the redesigned homepage, shared public header/footer, shared catalog primitives used by the product list (/products) and search (/search), product details (/products/:slug), cart (/cart), the shared form on both checkout routes, payment result (/payment-result), public tracking (/track-order) and personal orders (/my-orders and /account/orders). Checkout includes COD success and the online-created-order outcome with payment-link retry; payment result continues this system with distinct unconfirmed, authentication, pending, verified and retryable error states. Personal orders preserve both protected aliases. Authentication (/login, /register, /forgot-password and /reset-password) and account profile (/account/profile) now extend the same system with focused forms and truthful read-only owned information. Customer support chat now extends the public shell with the same emerald controls, flat white panel and truthful connection and sending states. Admin interiors remain future work. Global legacy tokens remain intact: root primary is Emerald-500, while these surfaces use its existing darker Emerald-700 token for readable controls and prices.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Concise Vietnamese copy
- Real catalog photography and prices
- Flat white surfaces and fine borders

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do preserve FoodDelivery, emerald/amber and Inter.
- Do show real catalog data and explicit loading, error, empty and unavailable states.
- Do keep mobile search and support accessible without covering purchasing controls.

- Don't replace API failures with purchasable sample products.
- Don't add unsupported ratings, certifications or delivery promises.
- Don't extend the shared shopping styling to remaining legacy interiors without reviewing them.
