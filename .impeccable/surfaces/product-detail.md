# Product detail

Mode: Operate. Code-led extension of the approved FoodDelivery homepage and catalog. User authorized the next step of the accepted plan after pushing completed catalog work. Retain real product data, reviews, Q&A and authentication/purchase rules. No new identity round, raster comp or roll is owed for the specified extension.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A product is understood through its own photograph, actual price, availability and a clear purchase action, rather than a fictional farm dossier.

**OWN-WORLD:** White/emerald/amber, Inter, fine slate rules and restrained rounded controls matching the approved catalog. One real photograph; no invented gallery, discount or certification badge.

**STORY:** Inspect the food, select a stock-safe quantity and add it. Read genuine descriptions/reviews and ask a question when authenticated. Show recoverable invalid, missing and load-error states.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Desktop splits a broad product photograph at left and the name, live price, stock, origin/category and purchase controls at right. Phone stacks photograph then purchase information. A short back-to-products link precedes it; description and compact review/Q&A tabs follow. No promotional hero or secondary bundle offer.

**FORM:** The same stock-aware selection and transient add-confirmation ring as the approved homepage; reduced motion suppresses the ring. Product-route changes reset local drafts; account changes reset community forms. Retain accessible product/Q&A/review selectors.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
